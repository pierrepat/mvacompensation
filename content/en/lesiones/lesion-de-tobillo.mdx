An ankle injury from a car accident can keep you off your feet for months. The ankle is a complex joint connecting three bones, multiple ligaments, and tendons — a strong impact can fracture bones, tear ligaments, or damage tendons. Settlements for ankle injuries range from $10,000 to $150,000 or more, depending on severity and whether surgery is required.

## Types of Ankle Injuries from Car Accidents

| Injury Type | What's Damaged | Recovery Time | Typical Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severe ankle sprain | Stretched or torn ligaments | 4-8 weeks | $8,000 – $20,000 |
| Single malleolus fracture | One ankle bone | 6-8 weeks | $15,000 – $40,000 |
| Bimalleolar fracture | Two ankle bones | 3-4 months | $30,000 – $80,000 |
| Trimalleolar fracture | Three ankle bones | 4-6 months | $50,000 – $150,000+ |
| Talus fracture | Bone connecting foot to leg | 3-6 months | $40,000 – $120,000 |
| Pilon fracture | Lower end of tibia | 6-12 months | $60,000 – $200,000+ |
| Achilles tendon rupture | Posterior ankle tendon | 4-6 months | $25,000 – $60,000 |

## Warning Signs After an Accident

- Severe pain when bearing weight
- Immediate and significant swelling
- Bruising extending to the foot and lower leg
- Visible deformity — ankle looks twisted or out of position
- Numbness or tingling in the foot (nerve damage)
- Popping or cracking sound at the time of impact

If you see visible deformity in your ankle after an accident, do not attempt to walk. A displaced fracture needs immediate immobilization.

## How Ankle Injuries Happen in Crashes

**Frontal collision** — your foot is on the brake pedal at impact. The force compresses the ankle against the vehicle floor, causing pilon and talus fractures.

**Side impact** — the vehicle floor deforms and traps or twists the foot, causing fractures and severe sprains.

**Rollover** — the foot can become trapped under pedals while the car rotates, causing multidirectional fractures.

## What Your Case May Be Worth

- **Severe sprain without surgery** — $8,000-$25,000
- **Simple fracture with cast** — $15,000-$40,000
- **Fracture with surgery (plates/screws)** — $40,000-$100,000
- **Complex fracture with complications** — $80,000-$200,000+

## Protecting Your Case

1. **Go to the ER the same day** — request ankle X-rays and a CT scan if complex fracture is suspected
2. **Follow treatment completely** — don't skip physical therapy even when you feel better
3. **Don't walk on a fracture** — use the crutches or boot prescribed. Walking too early can displace bones
4. **Document everything** — photos of swelling, medical bills, missed work days
5. **Don't accept the first offer** — read our guide on the [first insurance offer](/en/guias/primera-oferta-aseguradora)

## FAQ

**Can I claim for just a sprain, not a fracture?**
Yes. Severe sprains (grade II and III) can require months of treatment and cause permanent instability.

**What if I develop arthritis later?**
Post-traumatic arthritis is included in your claim as future damages. A physician can document the likelihood.

**Can I claim without health insurance?**
Yes. Many accident attorneys work with doctors who treat on a lien basis — they treat you now and collect when your case resolves.
