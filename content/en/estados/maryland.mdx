Maryland uses **pure contributory negligence** — any fault = potentially $0. Also caps non-economic damages at ~$920,000 (2026, adjusted annually). 3-year statute of limitations.

## Contributory Negligence

Any percentage of fault can bar entire recovery. A lawyer is essential.

## Statute of Limitations: 3 years

## Non-Economic Damages Cap: ~$920,000

Maryland caps pain and suffering. No cap on economic damages.

## Average Settlements

| Injury Type | Range |
|---|---|
| Soft tissue | $12,000 – $40,000 |
| Moderate | $40,000 – $130,000 |
| Serious | $130,000 – $350,000 |
| Catastrophic | $350,000 – $3,000,000+ |

## Insurance: 30/60/15 + $2,500 PIP
