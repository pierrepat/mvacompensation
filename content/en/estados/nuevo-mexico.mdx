New Mexico has the **highest percentage of Hispanic residents** in the country (~50%). Uses **pure comparative negligence** — you always recover something regardless of fault percentage (NMSA § 41-3A-1). 3-year statute.

## Pure Comparative Negligence

You always get something. 80% your fault = 20% of damages.

## Statute of Limitations: 3 years
