Tennessee has one of the most urgent laws in the country: the statute of limitations for personal injury is only **1 year** (Tenn. Code § 28-3-104). Half the time most states give you. Modified comparative negligence with strict 50% bar.

## Only 1 Year to Sue

12 months from the accident date. Miss it and you lose your right entirely. Act fast.

## 50% Bar

50% or more at fault = $0 (Tenn. Code § 29-11-103). Same as Georgia.

## Average Settlements

| Injury Type | Range |
|---|---|
| Soft tissue | $10,000 – $35,000 |
| Moderate | $35,000 – $125,000 |
| Serious | $125,000 – $350,000 |
| Catastrophic | $350,000 – $3,000,000+ |
